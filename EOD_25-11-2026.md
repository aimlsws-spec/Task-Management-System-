    - In `kick.py`, exported `should_scan()` checking `monotonic() - last_scan >= 60s`.
    - In `main.py`, HTTP request middleware kicks background runner on `GET` requests when `should_scan()` is true, ensuring background reminders fire seamlessly during day-to-day use.
