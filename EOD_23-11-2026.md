### C. Strict Edit and Delete Authority (Owner & CEO Only)
- **Security Policy:**
  - Only **Owner** and **CEO** have authority to edit or delete/cancel tasks and projects.
  - Admin, Head, SubHead, QA, and Employee cannot edit or delete/cancel tasks and projects, regardless of who created or received the assignment.
- **API Enforcement:**
  - `PATCH /tasks/{id}`: Restricted to Owner and CEO.
  - `POST /tasks/{id}/cancel`: Restricted to Owner and CEO.
  - `PATCH /projects/{id}`: Restricted to Owner and CEO.
  - `DELETE /projects/{id}`: Restricted to Owner and CEO.
  - `DELETE /projects/{id}/points/{pid}`: Restricted to Owner and CEO.
  - Non-leadership roles receive `403 Forbidden`.
- **Frontend UI Enforcement:**
  - Edit and cancel/delete buttons and controls on [TaskDetail.tsx](file:///c:/TMS/frontend/src/pages/TaskDetail.tsx) and [ProjectDetail.tsx](file:///c:/TMS/frontend/src/pages/ProjectDetail.tsx) are rendered strictly for Owner and CEO.
