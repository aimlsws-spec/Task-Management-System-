## 2. Technical Implementation Details

### A. Company Signup & Approval Architecture
- **Backend Modules**:
  - `backend/app/modules/org/companies_router.py`: `GET /api/v1/companies/public`
  - `backend/app/modules/org/admin_service.py`: `signup()` assigns company, sets `pending_approval`, and dispatches `EMPLOYEE_JOIN_REQUEST` to Owner and specific Company CEO.
  - `backend/app/modules/org/router.py`:
    - `GET /api/v1/employees/pending-approvals`: lists pending join requests scoped by caller's company authority.
    - `POST /api/v1/employees/{id}/approve`: promotes applicant to active, assigns department/manager, dispatches approval email.
    - `POST /api/v1/employees/{id}/reject`: deactivates applicant and dispatches rejection email.
  - `backend/app/core/deps.py`: `get_current_user` raises 403 `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` for `pending_approval` status.
  - `backend/app/modules/notifications/seed.py`: Seeded `EMPLOYEE_JOIN_REQUEST` notification rules for `ROLE:owner`, `ROLE:admin`, `COMPANY_CEO` (Rules version bumped to v7).
  - `backend/app/modules/notifications/resolver.py`: Added `COMPANY_CEO` recipient resolver querying `CompanyCEO` table.
- **Frontend Modules**:
  - `frontend/src/pages/Signup.tsx`: Dynamic company selection from `GET /api/v1/companies/public`.
  - `frontend/src/pages/PendingApproval.tsx`: Dedicated approval waiting screen.
  - `frontend/src/app/App.tsx`: Routing gate directing `pending_approval` users to the approval screen.
  - `frontend/src/components/PendingApprovalsCard.tsx`: Pending request cards with company badges and Approve/Decline actions.
  - `frontend/src/pages/Dashboard.tsx` & `frontend/src/pages/Employees.tsx`: Wired approvals card into Owner/CEO dashboards.
