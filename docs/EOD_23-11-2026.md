### C. Department SubHead & 4-Tier Fallback Loop
- **Role & Rank Hierarchy:**
  - Seeded role `sub_head` with rank **40** (`department_head` = 50, `employee` = 10).
  - Head (rank 50) can assign tasks downward to SubHead (rank 40).
  - SubHead (rank 40) cannot assign upward to Head (rank 50).
- **Department SubHead Management:**
  - Added `sub_head_employee_id` to `departments` table.
  - Implemented `PUT /departments/{dept_id}/subhead` API.
  - Updated [Departments.tsx](file:///c:/TMS/frontend/src/pages/Departments.tsx) table with SubHead column and setter action.
- **4-Tier Fallback Logic on Creation:**
  1. *Explicit Head & SubHead selected* $\rightarrow$ Both in loop.
  2. *Only Head selected* $\rightarrow$ Explicit Head in loop.
  3. *Only SubHead selected* $\rightarrow$ Explicit SubHead in loop as fallback.
  4. *Neither selected* $\rightarrow$ Automatically falls back to Assignee's department Head and SubHead.
  5. *Department has no Head/SubHead* $\rightarrow$ "Specific Employee Task" (skips intermediate head review stage, advances straight to QA or leadership).
- **Two-Tier Project Review:**
  - When all points/tasks finish:
    - **Tier 1 (Head / SubHead):** Department Head or SubHead clicks **"Review & Submit to Owner/CEO Panel"** (`head_reviewed_at`, `head_reviewed_by_user_id`, and `head_review_note` recorded; project remains active).
    - **Tier 2 (Owner / CEO):** Owner or authorized CEO gives executive approval and clicks **"Mark project completed"** (`status = 'completed'`).
  - Added respective review cards and status banner on [ProjectDetail.tsx](file:///c:/TMS/frontend/src/pages/ProjectDetail.tsx).
