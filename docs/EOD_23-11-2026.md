### M. CEO Task Completion & Direct Submission to Owner
- **Endpoint:** `POST /api/v1/tasks/{task_id}/submit-to-owner`
- As an allowed alternative to transferring an open task onward, the CEO can complete the work directly:
  - Fills out completion assurance summary and testing status.
  - State machine transitions task to `under_review`, `progress_pct=100`, and `review_stage="leadership"`.
  - Task is routed directly to the Owner's review queue.
  - Owner signs off and verifies completion via `POST /tasks/{id}/review` with `decision="approve"`.
- **UI Action:** "Complete & Submit to Owner" button appears in task header for CEO; displays `SubmitToOwnerPanel`.
