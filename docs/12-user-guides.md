# Document 12 — User Guides by Role

Sign in at the application address. Demo password for all demo accounts is shown in `SETUP.md`.

## Employee
1. **Dashboard (My Day)** lists new tasks to acknowledge, what to do next, deadlines and your workload.
2. Open a task → **Acknowledge** → **Start** (the server counts the time, so refreshing or closing the browser never loses it). Only one timer runs at a time.
3. Record **Progress**, **Put on hold** (with a reason), or **Report a blocker** (stops the clock; waiting time is kept apart from working time).
4. **Mark complete** with an assurance: what was done, whether it was tested, optional evidence.
5. **Overdue?** A red banner appears. You must give a reason, the work done, what remains, any blockers and an expected date. Your reason goes to your **Department Head and the Manager** for approval. Until they approve it, the CEO and Owner do not see it. If they ask for more or decline, send a newer explanation. To ask for more time, use **Request extension**.
6. Missing time? **Time** → request it; your manager approves.

## Department Head
1. **Dashboard** shows your department's open, overdue, blocked and on-time numbers.
2. Assign to anyone in your department (**New task**) and to **other Department Heads** (the Manager, CEO and Owner are told).
3. Open any department task and press **Take ownership** to record that you are accountable for it.
4. **Delays & Extensions**: read each employee's reason, then *Approve & share with CEO/Owner*, ask for more information, decline, approve a revised deadline, or escalate to leadership.
5. Verify or revert completed work; approve missing time; change deadlines (every change needs a reason; the original deadline never changes).

## Manager
Same as a Head for your whole team, including the Heads. You also approve delay reasons for staff who have no Head (Support, Sales, Accounts). Use **Team workload** before assigning.

## CEO and Owner
Assign to anyone (Owner: anyone; CEO: anyone up to CEO rank). The Dashboard shows the company: open/overdue/blocked, by department, by person. **You see an overdue reason only after the Department Head/Manager has approved or escalated it.** Reports → eight reports with CSV/Excel export. Owner/CEO can read the **Audit log**.

## Admin
Organization setup only: employees, departments, hierarchy, roles & permissions, settings, notification rules, audit log. The Admin never receives tasks.

## Reports and exports
Reports → choose a report, set dates/priority, view, then **CSV** or **Excel**. Everything is limited to the people you are responsible for. Completion, on-time, **active hours** and **elapsed time** are shown separately on purpose. Spreadsheet formulas in data are neutralised on export.
